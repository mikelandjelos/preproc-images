### Seminar 2 — image quality and preprocessing

Source: [professor's description](./seminar2.md). The professor allows text or images; **images** are the student's chosen branch.

- [ ] Register name, index number, **specific image topic**, and practical dataset in the `Drugi seminarski` sheet of the course document.
- [ ] Write a **20–25 page** theory document addressing the problem, relevant solutions, application conditions, foundations, strengths, weaknesses, and recommendations. Preserve an editable original and matching PDF.
- [ ] Cover relevant image quality factors: resolution, contrast, lighting, blur, noise, and compression artifacts.
- [ ] Cover appropriate preprocessing methods: resize, crop, normalization, and augmentation such as flip, rotation, and color jitter. Investigate their effect on a CNN model; choose a practical subset that fits the registered topic and dataset.
- [ ] Select an appropriate image dataset and demonstrate the discussed techniques in a Python Jupyter notebook with scikit-learn and other suitable libraries.
- [ ] Put the original theory document, matching PDF, and notebook in a public GitHub or Bitbucket repository; submit its link through the course forum.

For seminar 2, the professor does **not explicitly require** a baseline comparison, although a common baseline would make the effect of preprocessing clearer. For seminar 1, the baseline comparison is explicit.
